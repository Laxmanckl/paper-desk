## Skeptic memo

**H16 (weekend gap partial fill).** The strongest reason it fails: the backtest charges one fixed spread, but the real spread in the first hour after the Sunday reopen is often 3-10x normal, and the entry is in exactly that hour. A 75% chance rate with a half-gap target leaves little room. Second: "gaps fill" is the most repeated retail rule there is; if it worked cleanly it would be gone. Verdict: test, but only with the reopen bar charged at least 3x the normal spread, and judged on the chance line (75%), not on the win rate alone.

**H17 (gold Asia-hours drift).** The effect is documented, but much of the evidence is from years when Western selling around the London fix was strongest; it may have shrunk since the fix moved to an electronic auction (2015). Its best case is ~55% wins, far below the owner's 85% goal. Verdict: test (it is one of the few ideas here with a real mechanism), but expect a small edge at best.

**H18 (month-end dollar rebalancing).** Real flow, but the desk has no S&P 500 history, so it cannot be tested yet, and banks' own month-end models are hit-and-miss in public reviews. Twelve signals a year per pair means a long history is needed for any confidence. Verdict: test only once US500 daily history is exported; until then it stays an idea.
