## Idea 1: XAUUSD post-shock box breakout (daily)
- **Rule:** After a day whose absolute move is at least 3× the 20-day ATR, define a box once the next 5 or more daily closes all sit inside a band no wider than 2.5 ATR.
  - Go long on the first close above the box high plus 0.25 ATR, or short on the first close below the box low minus 0.25 ATR.
  - The stop is the opposite side of the box, capped at 2 ATR. The target is 2× the risk. Maximum hold is 15 days.
  - Run two variants: (a) trade both directions, and (b) trade only breakouts in the shock's direction.
- **Why it might work:** Large shocks are often followed by volatility compression. The breakout that ends the compression tends to carry because positioning has reset. The current gold box matches the setup almost exactly.
- **What would prove it wrong:**
  - Over at least 30 historical setups on gold (and EURUSD as a check), the expectancy after costs is ≤ 0R.
  - Or the win rate is below 35% at 2R.
  - Or the shock-direction variant does no better than the both-directions variant. That would mean the "shock" part adds nothing beyond an ordinary range breakout.

## Idea 2: EURUSD over-extension snap-back, with confirmation (daily)
- **Setup:** The close is at least 2.5% below the 50-day SMA, the 20-day change is ≤ -3% (at least about 12 average daily moves), and the 20-day closing low was set within the last 3 bars.
- **Entry:** Buy on the first daily close above the previous day's high.
- **Exit:** The stop is 1 ATR below the lowest low of the stretch. The target is the 20-day EMA or 2× risk, whichever is closer. Maximum hold is 10 days. Mirror the rules for upside stretches.
- **Why it might work:** EURUSD normally has low volatility. A one-way move of about 3.5 times the typical 20-day dispersion is rare, and partial reversion toward the 20-day mean is common once selling pressure pauses. Waiting for a close above the prior day's high keeps the trade out of a continuing slide.
- **What would prove it wrong:**
  - On at least 25 historical setups, the expectancy after costs is ≤ 0R.
  - Or more than half the trades hit the stop within 3 days, which would mean the stretch keeps extending instead of snapping back.
  - Or the results are no better than random entries inside the same stretched condition.
