## Macro Watcher memo

Searched the current backdrop: the dollar has firmed as traders scaled back 2026 Fed cuts, gold is off its highs, EUR/USD has slid 3.4% in 20 days.

The one macro flow that repeats on a schedule and is well documented is **month-end equity-hedge rebalancing**: foreign holders of US equities hedge the dollar exposure; when US stocks outperform in a month, their hedges must be topped up by *selling* USD into the last days of the month (and the reverse). Banks publish "month-end models" for exactly this. The edge is a forced, price-insensitive flow.

The desk cannot test it yet: the rule needs the month's US equity return (S&P 500), and the desk has no equity history. It needs an MT5 export of the broker's US500 daily bars first. I record it as an idea so it is not lost, flagged as blocked on data.

Nothing else I found is a repeatable rule rather than a forecast.
